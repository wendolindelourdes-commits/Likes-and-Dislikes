Cheat Sheet

Master Likes & Dislikes!

Learn when to add the magical 'S' and when to use Do / Does.

Group A (Plural & I/You)
I / WE

I, You, We, They

Verbs stay in their Base Form! No 'S' added.

Affirmative (+)

I / You / We / They like ice cream. 🍦

Negative (-)

I / You / We / They don't like spiders. 🕷️

Question (?)

Do you like music? 🎵

➜ Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Group B (Third Person Singular)
HE

He, She, It

Requires special attention! Remember the 'S'!

Affirmative (+) Add 'S'!

He / She / It likes pizza. 🍕

Negative (-) (Verb loses 'S')

He / She / It doesn't like broccoli. 🥦

Question (?) (Verb loses 'S')

Does she like cats? 🐱

➜ Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

The Golden "S" Rule! 🌟

In Affirmative sentences with He, She, It, add -s to verbs: likes, loves, hates, enjoys.
BUT when you use Does or Doesn't, the 'S' disappears from the main verb!
